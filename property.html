ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

Coastal living, your way.

Love Shack was created as an unpretentious luxury retreat where indoors and outdoors blend seamlessly. Expansive glass pocket doors invite the Caribbean trade winds into the airy great room, while shaded verandas keep afternoons comfortably cool.

Whether you are gathering for breakfast around the quartz kitchen island, swimming in the privacy of your garden pool, or taking three steps down to the warm turquoise water of Savaneta, every detail has been shaped for effortless island living.
