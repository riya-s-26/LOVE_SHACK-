ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

Coastal living, your way.

The Love Shack was created as an unpretentious luxury retreat where indoors and outdoors blend seamlessly. Expansive glass pocket doors invite the Caribbean trade winds into the airy great room, while shaded verandas keep afternoons comfortably cool.

Whether you are gathering for breakfast around the wooden breakfast island, relaxing in the open-plan living room with natural rattan seating and soothing ocean wave murals, or stepping directly down to the quiet sands of Savaneta, every detail has been shaped for effortless island living.

OUTDOOR SANCTUARY & GARDENS

The space

The gardens and surrounding gated property are equally stunning. Stepping out onto the terrace you will enter a comfortable gathering place with double sofa loungers, long seated chaise loungers, a dining area, a BBQ, and a huge private pool with outdoor shower… all overlooking the beach just a few steps away.

Pampered by the lush, well appointed indoor and outdoor spaces with a mix of sun and shade for lounging, dining, swimming and even early morning yoga practices under the palapa.... You will discover The Love Shack is the perfect spot to relax, soak up the sun, or enjoy evenings under the stars listening to the palms rustling in the warm breeze.

EXCLUSIVE PROPERTY PRIVILEGES

Guest access

You will be residing within our gated property, which houses our private pool with outdoor shower, covered patio, as well as uncovered patio area and the large palapa. Parking is also within the gated property. For those seeking adventure, we provide complimentary kayaks, paddleboards and snorkeling gear allowing you to experience the crystal-clear waters from your own beach. Explore right in your own backyard… just across the channel a short paddle away, lies Sea Glass Island, a magical spot for beachcombing and discovering colorful sea glass treasures unique to Aruba. Grab a plush, oversized beach towel, a beach chair and take to the beach!
