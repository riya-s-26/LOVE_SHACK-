THE DESTINATION

Savaneta — Aruba's authentic coast.

Far away from high-rise crowds, Savaneta is Aruba's oldest town and most charming seaside secret. Here, fishing boats glide past over calm turquoise lagoons, and world-renowned fresh seafood restaurants sit right on the sand.
